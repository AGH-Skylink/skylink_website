We’re excited to share a highlight from our student research group:  has recently developed a dedicated electronics board for the University of Aberdeen as part of our ongoing international cooperation.
The board is designed for an underwater exploration drone, a platform intended to simulate missions to subsurface oceans on worlds like Europa. Its purpose is to enable reliable communication between onboard sensors and the drone’s main control unit — a critical component for exploring extreme aquatic environments.
🚀 Collaboration, curiosity, and engineering — that’s what drives us forward.
